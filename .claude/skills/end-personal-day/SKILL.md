# End Personal Day (weekend/vacation evening, ~5-10 min)

You are running the user's personal day shutdown. Lighter than the workday version — personal days may have less "output" and that's perfectly fine.

---

## Step 1: Review the day

Read today's daily note — specifically `## Today's Options`, `## Intentions`, and `## Log` — to understand what was planned and what's been logged.

---

## Step 2: Capture accomplishments

Ask: "What did you get done today?"

Lighter framing than the workday version — could be errands, home projects, leisure milestones, social time, or simply rest. Cross-reference with today's `## Today's Options` items.

---

## Step 3: Advance the system

Same logic as end-workday, applied to whatever got done:

- **Daily note `## Today's Options`**: Mark completed items as `- [x]` in place. Copy completed items to `## Done Today` in the format `- [x] Action — [[Project]] — YYYY-MM-DD`. Undone items stay as `- [ ]` in Today's Options — they are historical record, not failures.
- **Project notes**: Find relevant projects (search by project name or `categories: ["[[Projects]]"]`) or `[[Action Pool]]`. Mark the corresponding action as complete (`- [x] ... — YYYY-MM-DD`), move to `## Completed` section. Update project context if the accomplishment changes the project's state.
- **New actions**: Add follow-ups surfaced by the day's activities.
- **Status changes**: Prompt if a project's last action is complete.

If nothing project-related happened, that's perfectly fine — skip this step gracefully.

---

## Step 4: Day-specific evening routines

Read `[[Day-Specific Routines]]`. Filter for today's evening items. Present applicable items.

---

## Step 5: Evening reflection

Guide through 4 prompts, write to `## Reflection` in today's daily note:

- "What went well today?"
- "Where did you feel the most friction?"
- "What did you appreciate most about today?"
- "How best can you spend your evening?"

---

## Step 6: Quick capture sweep

Ask: "Anything lingering that needs capturing?" If yes, capture to inbox or directly to the relevant project.

---

## Step 7: Commit

Commit all vault changes.

Note: No termination phrase — personal days don't need the same cognitive boundary as workdays. The reflection itself provides closure.
