# Getting Started

## Welcome to Weave

Weave is a productivity system where Claude acts as your organizational layer inside an Obsidian vault. There's no external task manager, no rigid workflow engine, no prescribed sequence of actions. Instead, Claude manages structure, surfaces context, and presents options weighted by usefulness and enjoyability. You make every decision.

The philosophical foundation is David Chapman's meta-rationality: use formal systems without being captured by them. Categories are provisional. Routines adapt. The orienting question is always: *"What could you do now that would be both useful and enjoyable?"*

For the full philosophical framework, see [[Weave - Chapman Framework]].

## Your First Three Skills

### /start-workday

Your morning review. Run this Tuesday through Friday (use `/start-work-week` on Mondays).

**What it does:** Creates today's daily note, checks in on how you're feeling, scans your calendar, reads all active projects, and collaboratively builds an action menu for the day.

**What to expect:** A ~10 minute conversation. Claude presents a dashboard of everything going on, then suggests options. You pick what goes on today's plate. Claude writes it all to your daily note.

See [[Your First Review]] for an annotated walkthrough.

### /create-project

Start a new multi-step initiative.

**What it does:** Creates a project note with a clear outcome, context, and an action menu (unordered checkboxes — you pick based on energy and interest, not a prescribed sequence).

**When to use it:** Any time you have something with 2+ actions and a clear desired outcome. Single standalone actions go to [[Action Pool]] instead.

### /process-inbox

Process captured items from all your inboxes.

**What it does:** Scans your Drafts inbox, Obsidian `Inbox/` folder, and optionally email. Pre-classifies each item, presents them grouped by action type, and asks for your confirmation before routing anything.

**How it works:** Claude does the sorting; you approve, override, or skip. Nothing moves without your say-so.

## What to Expect

- **Claude handles structure.** Frontmatter, linking, filing, category verification — you never maintain these manually.
- **Daily notes are your dashboard + journal.** Created automatically during reviews. Today's Options at the top, reflections at the bottom.
- **Reviews surface options.** Seven review sessions (from 5-minute shutdowns to 30-minute deep reviews) keep everything current. Claude presents what you *could* do; you choose.
- **Everything lives in the vault.** No external task managers. Projects, actions, reference material, routines — all in Obsidian.
